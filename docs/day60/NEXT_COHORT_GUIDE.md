# Guide for the Next AI Engineering Cohort

## Three Important Pieces of Advice

1. Build something small that works end to end before adding advanced features.
2. Test your system with realistic questions and record failures.
3. Document your work and share progress consistently.

## Two Mistakes to Avoid

1. Adding complexity before establishing a reliable baseline.
2. Treating a successful local demo as proof of production readiness.

## The Mindset Shift

Think in terms of systems and measurable user outcomes rather than isolated prompts or model calls. Data quality, retrieval, evaluation, reliability, and user experience all matter.

## Final Advice

Start on Day 1, keep your work reproducible, and treat every failure as evidence that can improve the next iteration.
