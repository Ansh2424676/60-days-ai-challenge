# Three Practical AI Engineering Opportunities

## 1. Resume and Job Matching
Job seekers spend significant time comparing their experience with job descriptions. HireLens can help extract skills from resumes, compare them with role requirements, identify gaps, and suggest improvements. The system should explain its recommendations, protect uploaded personal data, and avoid guaranteeing employment outcomes.

## 2. Knowledge Assistant for Learning
Students and professionals often struggle to find accurate answers across large collections of notes and documents. A RAG assistant can retrieve relevant passages, generate grounded explanations, and provide source references. Evaluation should measure retrieval relevance, answer correctness, and behaviour when the knowledge base lacks an answer.

## 3. Internal Document Support
Small organisations repeatedly answer questions about policies, processes, and documentation. A permission-aware document assistant can retrieve approved information and provide cited answers. The implementation should enforce access controls, protect sensitive documents, log failures appropriately, and route uncertain cases to a human.
