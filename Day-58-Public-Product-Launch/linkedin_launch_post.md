# Day 58 — Public Product Launch

## My AI Research Assistant

Over the past 60 days, I have been learning and building practical AI engineering projects as part of the ABTalks AI Engineering Challenge.

My AI Research Assistant explores how retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) can help an AI system retrieve relevant information before generating an answer.

### Architecture
- Python backend and APIs
- Retrieval and relevant-context selection
- Language model response generation
- Automated testing and evaluation

### Engineering challenge
A key challenge in RAG systems is ensuring that retrieved context is relevant and that generated answers are grounded in the available information. My approach is to test the system, inspect failure cases, and improve it iteratively.
