## Metric Definitions

- Total interactions: Actual interactions recorded by the application.
- Positive rating percentage: Positive ratings / total submitted ratings * 100.
- Errors: Actual API failures, exceptions, and unexpected user-facing behavior.

## Monitoring Schedule

- [ ] Initial baseline captured.
- [ ] First follow-up check completed.
- [ ] Subsequent checks recorded during the 24-hour period.
- [ ] Unexpected error patterns investigated.
- [ ] Final 24-hour totals recorded.
