# Day 57 — AI Product Pitch and Self-Review

## Version 1 — Initial pitch

Hello, I’m Ansh Shukla, and I’m building my skills in AI engineering through practical projects.

One of my projects is an AI Research Assistant. The goal is to help users find relevant information and get useful answers without manually searching through large amounts of content.

The core idea is retrieval-augmented generation, or RAG. The system retrieves relevant information from a knowledge base and uses that context to help a language model generate an answer.

My work in AI engineering has involved Python, FastAPI, retrieval pipelines, vector search, and automated testing. I have also explored ways to improve response handling, evaluation, and system performance.

One important challenge is that language models can produce answers that sound correct but are not sufficiently supported by evidence. To address this, I focus on retrieval quality, grounding answers in available context, and testing the system against representative questions.

I approach development iteratively: understand the problem, build a solution, test its behavior, identify weaknesses, and improve it based on evidence.

My goal is to make AI applications more reliable, useful, and practical for real users. I’m looking forward to applying these skills to more challenging AI engineering problems. Thank you.
